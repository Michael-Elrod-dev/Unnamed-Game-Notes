### Summary

Hex grid map about the size of one continent in Civ. Larger than the camera view, and far smaller than a world map.

### Rules

- Tiles carry a terrain type. Desert, grassland, and hills are the kind of thing meant, following Civ 6.
- Terrain is randomized per run. The premise of a location is fixed, and its layout is not.
- The player's starting position is auto placed.
- Structures can only go inside the city footprint. See [[City & Building]].
- Resource nodes sit outside the footprint. See [[Resources]].
