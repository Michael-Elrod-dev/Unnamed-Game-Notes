### Summary

Turn based combat on the hex grid, resolving close to Civ 6.

### Rules

- Units move a fixed number of tiles per turn.
- Sides alternate turns.
- Placement before the raid carries more weight than movement during it. Where the player set up is the decision the fight tests.

### Open

- **Open:** Damage model. Health pools, single roll resolution, or something else.
- **Open:** Melee and ranged rules, and whether ranged units need line of sight.
- **Open:** Whether terrain modifies attack or defense.
- **Open:** Whether units heal or are permanently lost after a raid.
