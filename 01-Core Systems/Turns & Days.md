### Summary

Everything is turn based. Turns group into days, and days count down to the next raid.

### Rules

- Actions per day are limited. Spending them advances to the next day.
- Units move a fixed number of tiles per turn.
- The next raid is visible to the player in advance. This is a requirement, not a preference. Frostpunk shows its storms and the whole game is preparing for a date you already know.
- Night is dead time. The player does not act at night.

### Open

- **Open:** How the countdown is displayed. A Frostpunk style timeline probably does not fit, since there is no night activity to fill it.
- **Open:** How many actions a day allows, and whether the limit is global or per unit.
- **Open:** Acting at night as an upgrade path, using specific units such as a night scout. Recorded as an idea, not a decision.
