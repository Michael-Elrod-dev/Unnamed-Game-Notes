### Summary

The city is a fixed footprint of hexes around the starting position. It does not grow with population the way a Civ city does.

### Rules

- Structures can only be placed on tiles inside the footprint.
- The footprint expands by purchasing an upgrade, not by growing a population.
- The fog stops a fixed distance outside the footprint, so pushing the boundary out also widens the battlefield ring. See [[Raid Sequence]].
- Nothing can be built outside the footprint. Resources can still be gathered outside it.

### Reference

![[FrostpunkMap.jpg]]

Frostpunk. Rings of buildings packed around one fixed central point, bounded on all sides by ground the player cannot use. Close to the footprint model, and worth noting that the density is what makes the city read as a place worth defending.

### Open

- **Open:** Whether the footprint upgrade is permanent across runs or purchased inside a single run. Permanent means every later run fights at a larger scale. In run means it competes with troops for resources. Both work and they produce different games.
- **Open:** Footprint starting size, and how much each upgrade adds.
