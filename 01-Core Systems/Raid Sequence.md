### Summary

The raid is the second half of the loop. The map collapses to a ring around the city and the player defends what they built.

### Rules

- The raid is announced in advance. The player always knows it is coming. See [[Turns & Days]].
- Camera centers on the city.
- Fog rolls in from the map edges and stops a fixed distance outside the city footprint.
- The band of open ground between the footprint and the fog is the battlefield.
- Anything standing in the fog when it arrives is lost immediately, workers included.
- Enemies emerge from the fog and advance tile by tile toward the city.
- Spawn direction is randomized. The player cannot solve the game with one fixed layout.
- Enemies target structures and units.
- Surviving the raid pushes the fog back out and returns the game to Build.

### Escalation

Raids get harder across a run. The final raid of a location is a boss, which is fought and not defended.

### Open

- **Open:** Whether the player can build or train while a raid is running.
- **Open:** How many raids a location holds before its boss.
- **Open:** Whether the fog advances further during a raid or holds its line.
