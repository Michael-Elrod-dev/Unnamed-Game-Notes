### Summary

Unity, 3D. Low poly, clean, stylized, and readable at a distance. Clash of Clans is the rough target for how finished it should feel.

### Constraint that drives everything

Nobody in the group is an artist. Assets get sourced from packs and kits, and the art direction has to be something reachable that way.

### Cost lives in animation

- Static structures are cheap. A model is a model.
- Every unit type needs idle, move, attack, and death at minimum.
- The practical consequence is fewer unit types carrying more upgrades, instead of a wide roster.

This is also the argument behind the shared archetype skeleton for races. See [[Units]].

### Per entity art

Each entity note carries a short Look section. Art does not get its own parallel folder tree. If a single entity's look ever needs more than a few lines, it can get its own note then.

### Open

- **Open:** Asset pack or kit choice.
- **Open:** Color palette and whether factions are separated by color, silhouette, or both.
- **Open:** Camera angle and how far the view sits from the board.
- **Open:** UI style.
