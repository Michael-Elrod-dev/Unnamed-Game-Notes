### Summary

Currency earned during a run that survives the end of it, win or lose, and buys permanent upgrades between runs.

### Rules

- Losing destroys the city and ends the run. Meta currency is the only thing carried out.
- Permanent upgrades apply to every future run.

### The flattening risk

Base builders are unusually sensitive to permanent economic bonuses. A permanent resource bonus does not ease the early game, it deletes it, and the opening of every run afterward becomes something the player clicks through. Whatever the upgrades end up being, this is the thing to watch.

### Open

- **Open:** What earns meta currency. Surviving raids, resources banked at the end, boss kills, or location completion.
- **Open:** The permanent upgrade list.
- **Open:** Whether the city footprint upgrade lives here or inside a run. See [[City & Building]].
- **Open:** Whether locations unlock through meta currency or purely by winning the previous one.
