Individual unit notes go in this folder, one per unit, using [[Unit]] from the templates. The roster is blocked on the setting, so nothing is named yet.

### Workers and soldiers are separate

Workers gather. Soldiers fight. They are different unit types and one does not do the other's job. Training an elven archer and then sending it to chop lumber does not hold up.

- Workers are cheap and replaceable. See [[Resources]].
- Soldiers are trained on purpose and cost real resources and time.

### Tiers

Soldiers come in tiers, following Civ. Basic units are available from the start. Stronger units unlock later and cost more resources and more time to train.

### Heroes

Heroes sit at the top of the roster. The player gets one per run, and it carries a field wide buff that shapes the whole build. This is the primary lever for making runs feel different from each other.

- **Open:** When the hero becomes available and what gates it. Current thinking is that the player cannot reach a hero early.
- **Proposal:** Choose the hero at the start of a run so it shapes play from turn one, with the run being about growing into it. This conflicts with the thinking above and has not been resolved.

### Races

Race choice inside a single theme is in scope. Choice across themes is not.

- **Open:** Whether the player picks a race, or plays one fixed race and varies the build inside it.
- **Proposal:** If races ship, give every race the same archetype skeleton, meaning a melee line, a ranged line, and a siege line at each tier. Identity comes from stats and one unique unit per race. Models are cheap and rigged animation is not, so sharing the skeleton is what keeps the cost survivable. See [[Art Direction]].
