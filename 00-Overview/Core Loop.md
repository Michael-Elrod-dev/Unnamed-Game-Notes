A run alternates between two states until the player wins the location or loses the city.

### 1. Build

- Assign workers to resource nodes out on the map. See [[Resources]].
- Spend resources on structures inside the city footprint and on training units.
- Actions per day are limited. Spending them moves the day forward.
- The next raid is always visible in advance. See [[Turns & Days]].

### 2. Raid

- Camera centers on the city.
- Fog rolls in from the map edges and stops a fixed distance outside the city footprint, leaving a ring of open ground.
- Anything caught in the fog is lost immediately.
- Enemies come out of the fog and advance tile by tile toward the city.
- Defenders are already placed. Placement is the decision that matters, because the attack direction is randomized.
- Combat resolves turn by turn. See [[Combat]].

Surviving pushes the fog back and returns the game to Build. Raids escalate across the run. The last raid of a location is a boss, which is fought.

### Why placement carries the game

Enemies do not always come from the same side. A watchtower covering one approach is what frees troops to cover another. The player should never settle into one correct layout.

The player must feel that their own decisions decided the fight. Avoid outcomes that read as an auto battler resolving itself.
