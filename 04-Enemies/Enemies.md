Individual enemy notes go in this folder using [[Enemy]] from the templates. Bosses go in the Bosses subfolder using [[Boss]]. The roster is blocked on the setting.

### Rules

- Enemies spawn out of the fog during a raid. See [[Raid Sequence]].
- Spawn direction is randomized between raids.
- They advance tile by tile toward the city and attack structures and units.
- Waves escalate across a run.

### Bosses

The last raid of a location is a boss. The boss is fought, not defended.

### Open

- **Open:** The enemy roster. Blocked on setting.
- **Open:** Whether enemies have tiers that scale alongside player tiers.
- **Open:** Whether a boss raid also carries normal waves or stands alone.
- **Open:** Whether enemies interact with terrain.
